# Isarva CRM User Guide

## 1. Introduction
Isarva CRM is a comprehensive customer relationship management system designed to help businesses manage leads, deals, organizations, contacts, and reporting efficiently.

## 2. Getting Started
- **Login:** Enter your credentials on the login page to access the dashboard.
- **User Roles:** Permissions are based on your role (Admin, Manager, Employee, etc.).
- **Navigation:** Use the sidebar to access modules like Leads, Deals, Organizations, Reports, and Settings.

## 3. Leads Management
- **Create Lead:** Go to Leads > Add New. Fill in details such as title, company, contact person, status, and expected close date.
- **Edit/Delete Lead:** Use the actions menu in the leads list.
- **Convert Lead:** Click 'Convert' to turn a lead into a deal.
- **Filter/Search:** Use filters for user, date range, and status.
- **View Details:** Click the lead title to see full details.

## 4. Deals Management
- **Create Deal:** Go to Deals > Add New. Enter deal details, link to company/contact, set status and deadline.
- **Edit/Delete Deal:** Use the actions menu in the deals list.
- **Stages/Status:** Track deal progress through stages and status.
- **Filter/Search:** Use filters for user, date range, and status.
- **View Details:** Click the deal title for more info.

## 5. Organization & Contacts
- **Add Company:** Go to Company > Add New. Enter company details.
- **Add Contact Person:** Go to Contacts > Add New. Enter first name, last name, and other details.
- **Linking:** Leads and deals can be linked to companies and contacts.

## 6. Reports
- **User-wise Reports:** View number of leads, converted leads, and deals per user. Click counts for detailed popups.
- **Filtering:** Filter reports by user and date range.
- **Export:** Download or print reports as needed.

## 7. Settings & Profile
- **Profile:** Update your profile and change password in the Settings section.
- **Roles & Permissions:** Admins can manage user roles and permissions.
- **Categories:** Manage product categories and tax rates.

## 8. FAQ & Troubleshooting
- **Forgot Password:** Use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page.
- **No Access:** Contact your admin if you lack permissions for a module.
- **Support:** For technical issues, contact support at support@isarva.com.

For more help, refer to the in-app tooltips or contact your system administrator.
