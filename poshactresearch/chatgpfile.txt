Complete POSH Flow Explained with Realistic Example

Let’s take a simple realistic office example and understand EVERY term and how the entire POSH software flow works.

Example Scenario

Suppose:

Company name = ABC Technologies
Employee = Priya
Manager = Rajesh
HR team exists
Company uses POSH software

One day Priya feels uncomfortable because Rajesh repeatedly:

sends inappropriate late-night messages
comments about her looks
asks her to meet alone outside office

This can fall under workplace sexual harassment.

Now let’s see how the software works step by step.

STEP 1 — Employee Opens POSH Portal

Priya logs into company HRMS/POSH portal.

She sees options like:

Raise Complaint
Anonymous Complaint
View POSH Policy
Contact IC Committee
Training Materials
What is POSH Policy?

This is company’s official anti-sexual-harassment policy.

Contains:

what counts as harassment
employee rights
complaint process
punishment possibilities
confidentiality rules

Usually uploaded as PDF.

STEP 2 — Complaint Filing

Priya clicks:

Raise Complaint

Now software shows complaint form.

Complaint Form Fields
Basic Details
Name
Employee ID
Department

OR anonymous option.

Against Whom?
Accused employee name
Department
Designation

Example:

Rajesh
Engineering Manager
Incident Details

Example:

On May 10th Rajesh repeatedly sent inappropriate personal messages
and insisted on private meetings despite refusal.
Incident Location
Office
Teams meeting
WhatsApp
Client location
Work trip
Evidence Upload

She uploads:

screenshots
emails
call recordings
chat exports

This is called:

Evidence Management

Software securely stores files.

STEP 3 — Complaint Submitted

Software creates:

Case ID: POSH-2026-0012

Now status becomes:

Submitted

Priya gets:

email confirmation
SMS/notification
acknowledgement receipt
STEP 4 — What is IC?

IC = Internal Committee

This is VERY important legally.

Indian POSH law says:
Companies with 10+ employees MUST have Internal Committee.

IC Usually Contains
Role	Meaning
Presiding Officer	Senior woman employee leading committee
HR Member	HR representative
Employee Members	Neutral employees
External Member	Outside legal/social expert
Why External Member?

Law requires external independent member.

Why?
Because company alone may hide cases.

External member ensures fairness.

STEP 5 — IC Receives Complaint

Now only authorized IC members can see complaint.

NOT:

random managers
unrelated HR
employees

This is:

Confidentiality Control

Very important.

STEP 6 — Initial Review

IC reviews:

is complaint valid?
enough information?
evidence available?
within legal timeline?

Possible outcomes:

Accept
Ask more information
Reject
Example

IC accepts Priya’s complaint.

Status changes:

Under Investigation
STEP 7 — Notice Sent to Accused

Rajesh receives formal notice.

Contains:

This is:

Notice Generation Workflow
